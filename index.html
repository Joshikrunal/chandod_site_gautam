Dakshini Prayag

Why families come to Chandod

Chandod sits at the Triveni Sangam, the confluence of the Narmada, the Orsang and the unseen Saraswati. Known as the Prayag of the South, it is one of the most revered places for Hindu families to perform rites for their ancestors.

Rituals performed here are believed to bring lasting peace to departed souls.
