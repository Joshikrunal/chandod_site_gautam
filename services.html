Rituals & poojas we conduct

Every rite performed according to Vedic tradition on the ghats of the Triveni Sangam, with guidance in Hindi, Gujarati or English.

Ancestral rites

For the peace of departed souls and the blessings of ancestors.

  • Narayan Bali

    Performed to liberate souls from untimely or unnatural death and grant them peace.

    Ask about Narayan Bali

  • Panch Bali

    Five offerings to cosmic beings, a core part of ancestral rites.

    Ask about Panch Bali

  • Pitru Shradha

    Offerings and prayers dedicated to ancestors for their peace and blessings.

    Ask about Pitru Shradha

After-death rites

Rites after a family member passes, performed in full.

  • Dashah Shradh

    Ten-day rites performed after death as per Vedic scripture.

    Ask about Dashah Shradh

  • Ekadashah Shradh

    The eleventh-day shradh, conducted with complete Vedic procedure.

    Ask about Ekadashah Shradh

  • Sampurna Uttar Kriya

    Complete post-death rites, performed in full from start to finish.

    Ask about Sampurna Uttar Kriya

Dosh nivaran & kundali

Horoscope reading and remedial poojas.

  • Kaal Sarp Dosh Nivaran

    Remedial pooja to relieve the effects of Kaal Sarp Dosh in a horoscope.

    Ask about Kaal Sarp Dosh Nivaran

  • Mangal Dosh Nivaran

    Rituals to pacify Mangal (Mars) dosh for marital and personal harmony.

    Ask about Mangal Dosh Nivaran

  • Kundali reading & dosh nivaran

    Horoscope reading and remedies for all types of kundali dosh.

    Ask about Kundali reading & dosh nivaran

हिन्दी

जय श्री कृष्ण — नारायण बलि, पंच बलि, पितृ श्राद्ध, कालसर्प दोष निवारण, मंगल दोष निवारण, कुंडली पठन, सभी प्रकार के कुंडली दोष निवारण, मृत्यु के बाद दशाह श्राद्ध, एकादशाह श्राद्ध, संपूर्ण उत्तर क्रिया तथा सभी प्रकार के धार्मिक अनुष्ठान कराए जाते हैं।

ગુજરાતી

જય શ્રી કૃષ્ણ — નારાયણ બલિ, પંચ બલિ, પિતૃ શ્રાદ્ધ, કાલસર્પ દોષ નિવારણ, મંગળ દોષ નિવારણ, કુંડળી વાંચન, તમામ પ્રકારના કુંડળી દોષ નિવારણ, મૃત્યુ પછીના દશાહ શ્રાદ્ધ, એકાદશાહ શ્રાદ્ધ, સંપૂર્ણ ઉત્તરક્રિયા તથા તમામ પ્રકારની ધાર્મિક પૂજા વિધિ કરાવવામાં આવે છે.

How to book

Booking takes one call

  1. Call or WhatsApp

    Tell us which ritual your family needs and roughly when you'd like to come.

  2. Confirm the muhurat

    Pandit ji confirms a suitable date and time, and tells you what your family should bring.

  3. Come to the Sangam

    Arrive at the Chandod ghats at the agreed time. Every step is guided on the day.
